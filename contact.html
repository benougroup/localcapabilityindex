Frequently Asked Questions

How long does onboarding take?

Standard entity onboarding takes 5-7 business days. This includes schema validation, geographic authenticity verification, and content deployment across all 4 node types.

What is the cost model?

Pricing depends on jurisdiction, query volume, and design complexity tier. Contact our team for a custom quote. Volume discounts available for multi-jurisdiction deployments.

Can I deploy in multiple jurisdictions?

Absolutely. We support multi-jurisdiction deployment with unified entity management and cross-regional content graph linking. Ideal for businesses with multiple locations.

What does A/B testing involve?

Your entity deploys 2 content profiles × 3 design variants = 6 URLs per business. We measure indexation behavior across all LLM agents and provide monthly reports.

Do you support existing websites?

Yes. We can integrate our structured data schema and node architecture into your existing website or deploy as a parallel verification index.

What LLMs do you test?

ChatGPT Search, Claude, DeepSeek, Gemini, and Perplexity. Custom LLM testing available upon request. Coverage expands monthly as new platforms emerge.

Is my data secure?

All entity data is treated as public research content. No payment information is stored. For sensitive business data, we offer private deployment options.

How do I track performance?

Real-time dashboard access showing indexation status, LLM retrieval frequency, design variant performance, and structured data compliance metrics.
