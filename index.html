How This Works

Unlike Google Maps (which only shows location), Local Capability Index connects three things:

  1. Your Problem: Describe what you're experiencing (e.g., "chronic ankle stiffness from old injury")
  2. The Solution: Understand what specialized treatment looks like
  3. Real Providers: Find qualified specialists who can help, with direct contact info

If a provider doesn't have their own website, they can use our platform. We're the middle layer connecting problems to solutions to companies.
