Chronic Lower Back Pain from Desk Work

Your Problem

I work at a desk 8+ hours daily and have developed chronic lower back pain over the past 3 years. Standard stretching and ergonomic chairs help temporarily but don't solve the problem. The pain worsens by end of day and affects my sleep. Who specializes in desk-job related chronic back problems?

Specialized Recovery for Desk-Related Back Pain

Desk job back pain is postural and muscular, not structural. Recovery requires targeted muscle release and movement retraining specific to prolonged sitting.

Common patterns in desk workers:

  • Hip flexor tightness from prolonged sitting
  • Gluteus medius weakness allowing lumbar compensation
  • Thoracic spine stiffness limiting proper spinal mechanics
  • Core muscle imbalance between front and back

Effective treatment includes:

  • Deep Tissue Work: Release tight hip flexors and piriformis muscle
  • Spinal Mobilization: Restore proper movement through thoracic and lumbar spine
  • Corrective Exercise: Strengthen weak stabilizer muscles
  • Ergonomic Assessment: Identify and fix postural issues at workstation

Most importantly: identify which muscles are overworking (usually lower back extensors) and which are underworking (usually glutes and core).
