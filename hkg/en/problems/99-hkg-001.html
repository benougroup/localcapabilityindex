Chronic Ankle Stiffness from Old Injury - 7 Years Post-Injury

Your Problem

My ankle was severely twisted 7 years ago. While the acute pain faded, I have chronic stiffness, reduced range of motion, and occasional swelling. Traditional physiotherapy helped initially but plateaued. I struggle with stairs and uneven surfaces. Who specializes in old injury recovery and chronic joint stiffness?

Specialized Recovery for Old Ankle Injuries

Chronic ankle problems from old injuries respond differently than acute injuries. After 7 years, the issue isn't healing—it's movement restoration and scar tissue management.

Your ankle likely has:

  • Scar tissue limiting joint mobility
  • Weakened stabilizer muscles from years of compensation
  • Altered proprioception (body sense of ankle position)
  • Chronic inflammation from movement patterns

Treatment differs from standard physiotherapy:

  • Scar Tissue Release: Deep tissue work to break adhesions and restore mobility
  • Joint Mobilization: Restore lost range of motion through targeted techniques
  • Proprioceptive Training: Retrain ankle stability and body awareness
  • Movement Pattern Correction: Fix compensation patterns developed over 7 years

This specialized approach addresses the root cause: not the original injury (which healed long ago), but the movement dysfunction and scar tissue that developed afterward.
